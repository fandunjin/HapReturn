Methods, interpretation and scope

Unique, traceable AGP provenance takes precedence. Accepted alignments fill intervals of unknown provenance only when exactly one original source supports the interval. Conflicting evidence remains visible and is flagged for review; a chromosome-level label does not remove minority-source components. AGP and minimap2 derive from related sequence inputs and are not independent statistical replicates.

The figures describe the selected chromosome-scale records of one assembly. Unplaced sequences remain in the exported FASTA files. Coordinate tracks are in megabases; interval tables use 1-based inclusive coordinates. Values are descriptive measurements, with no inferred uncertainty intervals or hypothesis tests. Source recovery does not establish parental phase continuity.

Short intervals can fall below the display resolution; exact lengths remain available in the evidence profile and source tables. Scientific figures are exported as editable vector graphics.
